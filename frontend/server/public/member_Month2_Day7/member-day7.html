Week 1 Celebration: "Body Word Parade"

How to Do It 5 minutes

  • Touch your nose together -- say 'nose!'
  • Touch your ears -- say 'ears!'
  • Touch your tummy -- say 'tummy!'
  • Do a little dance: clap, jump, wave
  • Say: 'We know all those words!'

What You Say

"Where's your nose? There it is!"
"You know so many body words!"

Parent Note

Week 1 was about the most fundamental vocabulary of all: the body your child lives in. Every word they learned this week is a word they carry everywhere, every day. The words for body parts become the words for feelings ('my tummy hurts'), for needs ('I need a hug'), and for self-awareness ('I'm tired'). You didn't just teach vocabulary. You taught them to talk about themselves.
