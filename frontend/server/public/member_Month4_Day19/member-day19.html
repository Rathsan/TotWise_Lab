Age 2--3 Milestone Having a designated calm spot -- a safe physical space to regulate -- is one of the most practical and evidence-backed tools for toddler emotional regulation. It teaches self-directed soothing.
🏠

Today's Play: "My Calm Spot"

Setup 30 seconds

  • Choose a corner together
  • Make it cosy: blanket, soft toy, the calm jar

How to Play 10--15 minutes

  • Name it together: Our Peace Place
  • Show what we do there: breathe, squeeze something soft, count
  • Practise going there BEFORE you need it
  • Go there when big feelings start

What You Say

"This is your peace place -- it is yours when you need it."
"We go there to help our feelings settle -- not as a punishment."
"How does it feel in the peace place? Different from the rest of the room?"

If This Happens...

Child refuses to go to calm spot when upset

Avoid saying

"Go to your calm spot NOW."

Say this instead

"I am going to the peace place -- want to come with me? You do not have to stay. Just try it."

Why this works: A designated regulation space works because spatial anchoring is a powerful learning mechanism. The brain creates strong context-dependent memories, so a space consistently used for calm eventually triggers a calming response on its own. This is why therapists use consistent rooms and why patients often feel calmer just entering a familiar therapeutic space. Building this association early gives children a lifelong tool.
