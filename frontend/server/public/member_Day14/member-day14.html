Week 2 Celebration: "Feeling Parade"

How to Do It 3 minutes

  • Take turns naming a feeling and making that face
  • Start: "I'm going to look happy!" — make a big happy face
  • Point to your child: "Your turn — what face?"
  • Try happy, silly, surprised, sleepy

What You Say

"That's your happy face!"
"Two weeks — look how far we've come."
