Today's Play: "Sit and Listen"

Setup 30 seconds

  • A story or short structured activity
  • Set a clear frame: 5 minutes, then free play

How to Play 10--15 minutes

  • 5-minute story with a clear beginning, middle, end
  • Child sits and listens -- no toys
  • Ask 2 questions after
  • Celebrate: 5 minutes of focused attention

What You Say

"For 5 minutes: just sit and listen. Then you can do anything."
"After the story: what happened first?"
"5 minutes of attention -- that is a real skill."

If This Happens...

Child cannot sit for 5 minutes

Avoid saying

"Sit still -- I said sit."

Say this instead

"OK, 2 minutes first. We build up to 5. Ready? 2 minutes -- the story is short."

Why this works: Sustained attention capacity -- the ability to maintain focus on a task for a specified period -- is one of the three most consistent school-readiness predictors (along with self-regulation and vocabulary). Building attention in gradual increments (2 minutes, then 3, then 5) follows the evidence-based approach of attention training research. The post-story questions activate comprehension monitoring -- the habit of listening actively rather than passively.
