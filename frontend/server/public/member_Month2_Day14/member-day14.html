Week 2 Celebration: "Colour & World Walk"

How to Do It 5 minutes

  • Walk through one room together
  • Point to something and ask: 'What colour?'
  • Let child name the colour -- any colour
  • Find 3 colours together
  • End with: 'You know so many words!'

What You Say

"What colour is this?"
"Red! You found it!"
"You know so many words about the world!"

Parent Note

Week 2 took your child's language outside their own body and into the environment. Colour words, size words, food words, nature words, and the first two-word combinations. The jump from one word to two words is not a small step -- it is the beginning of grammar. When your child says 'big ball' instead of just 'ball', they are not just using more words. They are building a sentence.
