Age 2–3 Milestone: 30-Day Milestone Children who complete a month of structured, daily developmental play with a present caregiver show measurable improvements in language, social skills, and emotional regulation. But the most important thing built this month is the relationship — the pattern of your child turning to you, and finding you there.
🌟

Today's Activity: "Our Story So Far"

Setup 5 minutes

  • Find a blank piece of paper or open a note on your phone
  • Sit with your child in a comfortable spot
  • You are going to tell your child a true story — the story of this month

How to Do It 5–10 minutes

  • Say: "Let me tell you what we did together this month."
  • Tell them one activity you remember — any one. Make it specific.
  • Say: "Do you remember?" (They may not — that's fine. You remember.)
  • Then draw one thing from the month together — anything: a tower, a bowl, a face
  • Write or say: "We did 30 days. Together."

What You Say

"We learned so much together."
"I am so proud of you."
"We did 30 days. That's us."
