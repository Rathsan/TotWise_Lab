Today's Play: "Poetry Time"

Setup 30 seconds

  • A short poem -- any age-appropriate poem will do

How to Play 10--15 minutes

  • Read it once straight through
  • Read it again, emphasising rhythm
  • Ask: what pictures does this poem make in your mind?
  • Memorise the first two lines together

What You Say

"Listen to the music of these words..."
"What do you see in your mind when I say that?"
"Let us memorise two lines -- say them with me."

If This Happens...

Child says poems are boring

Avoid saying

"Give it a chance -- it gets better."

Say this instead

"Tell me what a poem would sound like if you made it. What would it be about?"

Why this works: Literary language -- the elevated, condensed, rhythm-driven language of poetry -- builds a different kind of linguistic sensitivity than conversational language. Children exposed to poetry develop stronger metaphor comprehension, richer vocabulary, and greater sensitivity to the emotional and aesthetic qualities of language. These are not just literary skills -- they are reading comprehension skills that differentiate proficient from struggling readers.
